## 5. TLS without paid domain — exact labor and operational fragility
[Let's Encrypt Jan15 2026](https://letsencrypt.org/2026/01/15/6day-and-ip-general-availability) supports **trusted IP-address certificates** free to issue, always **short-lived 160 hours**. [Certbot Mar11 2026](https://letsencrypt.org/2026/03/11/shorter-certs-certbot) says use **Certbot >=5.4** for webroot IP validation, `--preferred-profile shortlived` and `--ip-address`; **it does NOT automatically install the IP cert into Nginx/Apache**, requires explicit cert/key paths, a deploy-hook to reload reverse proxy, automatic renewal and public HTTP challenge accessibility; [Let's Encrypt FAQ](https://letsencrypt.org/docs/faq/) recommends renewal every **3 days** for six-day certificates. The idea of "free HTTPS" is feasible at document level but **not unattended proof**.
**Constraints:** OCI public IPv4 (ephemeral vs reserved) assignment and **actual invoice** unverified; IP can change on VM replacement, requiring a new cert, updated user URLs/cookie origin, browser checks. HTTP-01 requires inbound 80 reachable and gateway/firewall rules; 443 stable for application; 3306 never public. Renewal monitoring, deploy-hook and alert failure must be tested. Free host name via third-party DNS may have terms/reliability and is **not assumed available**.

## 6. Off-provider recovery: new usable $0-on-paper candidate and hard limits
**Verified external:** [Backblaze B2 published price](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/pricing) grants **first 10 GB free**; official [Backblaze B2 signup help](https://help.backblaze.com/hc/en-us) says no billing method is required to start. [Backblaze caps](https://www.backblaze.com/docs/cloud-storage-data-caps-and-alerts) can cap spending on storage and Classes A–C, **NOT Class D**, and no caps = charges can accrue; [caps edit docs](https://www.backblaze.com/docs/cloud-storage-create-and-manage-caps-and-alerts) says changes may take up to 10 minutes. Thus free allowance and no initial billing method do **not** prove hard automatic $0 lifetime under every usage condition. Need inspect actual account caps and charge behavior before calling eligible.
**Client-side encryption** before upload is **recommended** even for fictional member/account demos. [Backblaze security docs](https://www.backblaze.com/docs/cloud-storage-security) encourage own crypto, while server-side SSE encrypts object bodies but **metadata isn't encrypted** per [B2 SSE](https://www.backblaze.com/docs/cloud-storage-server-side-encryption). Store encryption keys in a separately controlled offline medium (not only same OCI tenant or Backblaze bucket), verify checksum/hash manifest and chain of custody. Avoid real organization or student data at research/demo stage.
**Cloudflare R2 contrast:** [R2 getting started updated Nov12 2026](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/get-started/) requires **R2 subscription checkout**; [R2 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/) offers free standard 10 GB-month but charges above free storage/ClassA/ClassB operations. [Cloudflare marketing page](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/r2/) says "no credit card required" while its official onboarding says checkout—**not a contradiction proving charges**, but account-specific payment/overage cannot be assumed. Without hard $0 spending cap, not preferred as DR for this strict zero-mandatory-fee use.
**Same-provider OCI Object Storage** (20GB Always Free-only) and free block-volume backups can be useful near-term copies but are in the **same OCI tenancy**, vulnerable to account disable/tenancy lock/provider outage. They are **not independent off-provider disaster recovery**.

## 7. Database export/recovery acceptance invariant — neither route is presently proven
**Verified source:** [MySQL8.4 mysqldump](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/mysqldump.html) uses `--single-transaction` for **InnoDB-only consistent snapshot**, `--quick` for streaming; **no concurrent ALTER/CREATE/DROP/RENAME/TRUNCATE** while dumping. Include routines/events/triggers if configured, session/auth tables, migrations/version history, immutable movements, recovery code hashes and audit event offsets. Dumping encrypted secret keys in database is forbidden; keep operational keys separately, rotate after recovery. For HeatWave 9.7, check [Oracle MySQL Shell export/import](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/mysql-database/doc/overview-exporting-and-importing.html) and compatibility validation before assuming 8.4-style dump/restore.
**DESIGN RECOMMENDATION:** Offsite job daily on test data: produce consistent dump under controlled migrations, compress, client-side encrypt (authenticated encryption), SHA-256 manifest with schema version/DB version/resource/membership/ledger counts and UTC cutoff, upload to independent provider under small bounded quota, verify from outside OCI, rotate copies; re-import into **clean local MySQL** (not the one source), compare invariants and record RPO/RTO. Avoid displaying passwords, sensitive receipts or private member phone numbers; seed fictional only.
**Critical RPO/RTO truth:** Proposed targets **RPO <=24h, RTO <=48h** are **ASSUMPTIONS**. A one-day vendor backup retention might not give a usable 24h recovery after an outage coincides with deletion; unavailable free hosting may prevent achieving 48h. "We have a dump" != "we can restore service" until actually measured. An offline user-controlled encrypted backup is a no-new-provider-bill fallback (nonzero device/electricity costs), but not guaranteed unattended cloud uptime.
**Security:** no application publicly exposed until account, HTTPS cert/renewal and real tenant/auth/CSRF/race/backup checks passed.
