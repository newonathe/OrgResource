# DEC-D01 — Department affiliation, resource discovery, and project-demand visibility

**Recorded:** 2026-11-23 Asia/Manila. **Status:** USER PRODUCT INTENT CAPTURED; DESIGN RECOMMENDATION / PROPOSED DECISION, NOT YET ACCEPTED FOR IMPLEMENTATION. **Authority:** User requested that organization members have department-specific resource visibility while being able to discover other departments' offerings and their own project requirements and requests. This file preserves that request, evaluates the design alternatives, and specifies a compatible small-scope recommendation. It does **not** mean the user approved every recommended detail, an Ateneo process was verified, or code/migration already exists.

## User's proposed behavior (intent preserved)

- A person joins an organization / department, can see the resources belonging to their department, may see the offerings of other departments, and can inspect which items a Project needs, requests, and associated status/history.
- The actual question to resolve is whether the person must belong to **exactly one organization and department** or should have an organization membership with a primary department, and what may be shared cross-department.
- This is a prototype workflow hypothesis; there are no verified Ateneo department custody rules, participation data, campus integrations, or institutional permission.

## Alternatives considered

**A — Exactly one organization and one department per login:** simple to explain, but incompatible with the current multi-organization Membership model and the 96-workspace demo owner; officers who participate in two organizations or departments would need duplicate accounts. **Reject as a global restriction.**

**B — Organization membership with one primary department and role/Project-based access (RECOMMENDED MVP):** each real MEMBER/LEAD normally has exactly one *primary department per organization* once assigned, while OWNER/ADMIN/CUSTODIAN may operate organization-wide and members may have assigned Project access outside their primary department. Existing unassigned accounts stay usable during migration. A department has a default **resource steward** rather than a separate stock ledger. Read-only cross-department catalog entries show enough information to request items, without exposing transaction-level details. **Recommend.**

**C — Many departmental memberships, distributed inventory pools and interdepartment transfers:** appropriate only for verified cross-department operating workflows; entails transfer approval, reservation ownership, reconciliation and more roles. **Defer.** If multiple departments per person are demonstrated as necessary later, add an explicit \`department_membership\` relationship instead of encoding comma-separated departments.

## Proposed domain contract (V1, pending user approval)

1. **Identity:** \`user_account\` = login; \`membership(org_id,user_id,role,status,primary_department_id NULL)\` = organization role plus optional home department. A user can have **0..N organizations**; a membership has **0..1 primary department**. The \`NULL\` state supports existing accounts, bootstrap OWNERs, synthetic demos and pending invitations; a regular real member should be assigned before accessing the department dashboard (controlled onboarding \`DEPARTMENT_ASSIGNMENT_REQUIRED\`, not data corruption). The proposed FK \`(primary_department_id,org_id)\` → \`department(id,organization_id)\` must have its referenced composite index confirmed. Assign/replace/clear only under an authorized organization OWNER/ADMIN; track who/when in audit; revoke stale access on reassignment.
2. **Resource stewardship:** add nullable \`resource.steward_department_id\` with same-organization FK. \`NULL\` is **Shared Organization Inventory**. Stewardship indicates who manages the catalog item; physical resource totals, stock movement, approvals and issued balances remain governed by the existing organization-scoped InventoryService. **One item/one stock pool** in MVP: neither duplicate resources per department nor treat labels as physical locations or custody changes. Optional physical \`current_location\` is out-of-scope until validated.
3. **Discoverable catalog (inside the same active organization only):** allow eligible active members to read a **minimized catalog card** for each eligible resource: \`resourceId, name, type, stewardDepartmentName or "Shared", requestable, availableQuantity? \` as a server-derived, time-sampled informational value. No contacts, borrower identities, audit records, internal provider details, member directory, raw movements or cross-organization records. Never promise stock availability for a Project solely from current quantity; revalidate at approval/issue under concurrency and scheduled reservations. Showing quantity to every active member is an **OPEN QUESTION**; a simpler initial policy is availability bands or "Check availability" for foreign departments.
4. **My Department (private operational view):** display resources stewarded by the member's primary department and that department's Projects, Requirements and own actionable requests, with visibility at most as broad as the user's actual Project assignment/role. Department affiliation alone grants **catalog reads**, not authority to approve, issue, edit, cancel another user's request or see other borrowers. OWNER/ADMIN/CUSTODIAN keep explicitly documented organization-wide operations. For LEADs, preserve assigned-Project restrictions; do not silently replace them with blanket access to all department Projects.
5. **Cross-department "Offerings" (read-only):** members can browse catalog cards stewarded by other departments, then add an eligible Resource to an authorized Project's Requirement and submit a PENDING Allocation by existing approved paths. The Project's department remains its \`project.department_id\`, even if the Resource steward is a different department. A cross-department request **does not itself transfer stock or grant stock-custodian permissions**. If separate department approval is desired, specify that as a **new, optional workflow** with explicit state transitions; it is not automatically supported by current Allocation states.
6. **My Project Needs/Requests:** for a current authorized Project, show \`projectId, name, owningDepartment, startAt, endAt\`, Requirements \`requirementId, resourceId, resourceName, neededQty, coveredQty, shortageQty\`, and authorized Allocations \`allocationId, quantity, status, relevant timestamps\`. "Needs" are Requirements; "Requests" are Allocation records; "Offerings" are Resource catalog items; the three must not be conflated. Allow the assigned LEAD and officers to see appropriate Project request summaries, and each borrower/requester to see only their own minimal records. Other departments' project detail is **not** publicly readable merely because the department offers a resource.
7. **Policy dimensions:** every read/write checks \`active account + active org membership + same-org Department/Project/Resource + role + primaryDept when relevant + assignedProject or borrower/requester link\` inside server services and SQL. Mask foreign and unassigned Project IDs with 404; use 403 for known-role denied operations where existence is already safe; appropriate validation/409 for missing home department. Apply scoping to paging/cursors, exports, images, dashboards, deep links, legacy routes, and **existing organization-wide list methods** (which must not remain bypasses). Control query leakage and stale cache after reassignment. OWASP API1:2023 requires object authorization on each ID-based operation (https://api-security.owasp.org/editions/2023/en/0xa1-broken-object-level-authorization/). NIST SP 800-162 describes evaluating subject, object and action attributes (https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/162/final).
8. **Demo migration:** do **not** alter the SHA-approved 96-organization plan, importer, source roster, or original account/credentials to invent home departments. Any Extended-C schema change must use a new additive Flyway migration after V7, with nullable fields first and carefully reviewed backfill where evidence exists. The current hosted C8 preflight requires **exact V7**; it will need deliberate compatibility review before schema upgrades. Treat the current P0 demo and current draft PR as a separate safety-gated workstream, and do not blend this proposal into the active live import.
9. **Boundaries:** no institutional SSO, ADMU org validation, cross-organization borrowing, automatic interdepartment financial settlement, asset-serial tracking, transfer workflow, or new SMS/contact collection without a separate decision. No claim about how real student organizations operate.

## Example, strictly fictional

Within fictional Organization A, a Media member's *home department* is Media. The Project "Demo Campus Event" belongs to Media and requires two microphones. Logistics stewards the organization-wide "Demo Audio Kit" Resource. The Media member can see the Logistics offering card. An authorized Project LEAD adds a Requirement for two units; the permitted request flow creates a PENDING Allocation bound to the Project/Requirement. CUSTODIAN later approves against physically available, uncommitted stock. The Media member sees their own request status; they **cannot** see Logistics' other borrowers, private movements or unrelated Projects. The source department still owns catalog stewardship, but the ledger remains organizational.

## Acceptance tests and phased implementation backlog

**Phase D1 — decision + model (currently this document only):** confirm whether members must have one primary department; whether foreign-department availability shows exact quantities or bands; whether all Projects in a home department are visible in summary or only assigned Projects; who approves cross-department requests. **No code exists yet for these rules.**

**Phase D2 — additive schema and policy:** nullable scoped department assignment for Membership, resource steward dept FK, server-owned assignment mutation (OWNER/ADMIN) with audit; explicit \`Shared\` option; preserve current original users and private demo provenance. Verify composite FKs, data migrations, null behavior, account/membership lifecycle and no changes to frozen Course A. Keep it on a **separate branch after P0**.

**Phase D3 — read APIs + UI:** bounded \`GET /api/v2/orgs/{orgId}/catalog?departmentId=&search=&limit=&cursor=\` returning minimized catalog; \`GET /api/v2/orgs/{orgId}/my-department\` returning own scope; reuse/extend existing Project/Requirement/Allocation operations rather than duplicate lifecycle endpoints. React views **My Department**, **Organization Catalog**, **Project Needs & Requests** with clear resource origin badges, role-specific actions and separate loading/empty/denied states. Route names, DTOs, pagination and error contract are **proposal**, not current API.

**Phase D4 — refusal and consistency proof:** HTTP+MySQL test with two orgs, three departments, shared resource, two Projects, different owner/admin/custodian/LEAD/member accounts, alternate borrower and suspended member. Require own-department visibility; foreign department **catalog only**; assigned Project success; unassigned/foreign Project 404; denial of other borrower details; wrong-org ID refusal; cross-department request uses existing stock safety; source and target totals unchanged on denied writes; department reassignment immediately revokes prior access; pagination/cursor and legacy read/export/image routes cannot leak. Include concurrent approvals and simulated source stock shortage; owner access to all 96 demo orgs remains unaffected.

**Phase D5 — optional later:** formal resource transfers, multi-department users, full department approvals, location/custody tracking, organization-to-organization lending, and department-level analytics. Require actual workflow evidence and explicit approval before growing scope.
