## Falsifiable acceptance conditions for any public C8 demo

1. Every rendered org identity has `reference_key`, `institution_kind`, evidence year, URL, and a clear badge/heading like `DEMO — SIMULATED ACTIVITIES; NO AFFILIATION/ADOPTION IMPLIED`; open source URL only as a factual reference.
2. Every generated project title, borrower/persona, resource, issue/return/consumption and stock metric is fictional and repeatable from a deterministic seed/fixture manifest. Actual hosted UI, API JSON/export and screenshots show same source-versus-simulation boundary.
3. No real student name, member list, photos, social media handles, phone or email, real financials/budgets, exact event transactions or association endorsement are seeded. MISA and Sanggunian heavier **mock load is a design choice, not a claim they are Ateneo's largest organizations**.
4. One extra org account/session may not read or write another tenant, including named flagship MISA ↔ Sanggunian; immutable audit and stock balances reconcile after 1000s of cycles.
5. Full 96-org source roster browsing, historic pages and exports are bounded/server-paginated and correctly categorized. Source changes and removed orgs are versioned, never retroactively rewritten into old stock-movement facts.
6. Actual measured import/backup/restore and public read-only demo guest access are separately approved and tested; no hosted product or importer has been verified as existing at C6.
